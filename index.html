Hello 👋

I’m a graduate student at the MIT Media Lab and a Research Affiliate at the Stanford Digital Economy Lab (HAI). I study networked systems of humans and AI agents — how they coordinate, how they make decisions, how detectable and steerable they are — and what that tells us about the future of social networks, digital markets, and hybrid organizations.

Before that, I worked as a software and ML engineer at two AI startups, where I trained language models and built production pipelines around them; I received my Bachelor’s degree from Columbia University, as its first Data Science + Psychology double-major; and I produced music for many years from my bedroom in a tiny, rural town in Israel.
